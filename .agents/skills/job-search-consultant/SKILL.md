# Job Search, Resume & Career Consulting Skill

This skill provides an end-to-end framework for job search strategy, resume writing, cover letter creation, networking, ATS optimization, and AI prompt engineering using proven executive job search and ATS optimization playbooks.

Whenever discussing job searches, resume reviews, application strategies, cover letter drafting, or interview prep, follow the core methodologies outlined below.

---

## 1. The Core 3-Step Job Search Philosophy

To land any given job, **3 things must happen**:

```
1. Uncover Employer Requirements (Market Research / Job Posting Dissection)
                       ↓
2. Prove You Meet or Exceed Requirements (Resume, Cover Letter, LinkedIn, Interview)
                       ↓
3. Earn Decision Maker Trust (3TC Networking & Referral Strategy)
```

---

## 2. Dissecting Job Postings (Market Research)

Before writing any resume or cover letter, dissect the target job posting into two categories using the **Dissecting Job Postings Matrix**:

| **Job Duties & Hard Skills** (Primary Focus) | **Professional Traits & Behaviors** (Culture / Soft Skills) |
| :--- | :--- |
| • Technical proficiencies & tools (e.g., Python, SQL, HRIS, Salesforce) | • Work environment fit (e.g., fast-paced, high-stakes, collaborative) |
| • Key responsibilities & deliverables (e.g., revenue generation, budget oversight) | • Interpersonal traits (e.g., empathetic, detail-oriented, resilient) |
| • Required education, certifications, and licenses | • Mindset (e.g., process-oriented, customer-obsessed) |

### The 80% Rule
Candidates **do not need 100%** of the required qualifications. Having ~80% of the required skills and experience is more than enough to apply with confidence.

## 3. Building High-Impact Resume Bullet Points

Every high-impact bullet point follows a **3-part formula**:

$$\text{High-Impact Bullet Point} = \text{Action Verb} + \text{Specific / Quantifiable Detail} + \text{Measurable Result}$$

### The 3 Components
1. **Action Verb**: Strong, punchy starting verb (e.g., *Spearheaded, Streamlined, Directed, Oversaw, Generated, Negotiated, Optimized*). Avoid weak verbs like "Worked on" or "Responsible for".
2. **Specific / Quantifiable Detail**: Scope, scale, frequency, team size, budget size, software used, or client base volume.
3. **Measurable Result**: The outcome achieved (e.g., *increased retention by 45%, reduced processing turnaround by 88%, saved $300K/year*).

### Non-Negotiable Bullet Rules
- **Never Lie / Fabricate Data**: If exact numbers are unknown, use qualitative metrics (*"yielded above-expected retention rates"*) instead of making up fake stats.
- **Protect Confidentiality**: Swap company names for industry descriptions (*"supported a major client in automotive"*) and dollar figures for percentages or ranges (*"managed a 7-figure budget"*).
- **Tense Rules**: Past tense for previous roles and completed projects; present tense for current ongoing/recurring duties.
- **Length**: 1-3 lines per bullet point.

## 4. Resume Structure & Formatting Guidelines

### Professional Branding (Header & Top of Page 1)
1. **Headline / Title**: Clearly match the target job title or expertise (*"Forensic Accountant"*, *"Accounting Expert"*, or *"Relationship-Oriented Leader"*).
2. **Professional Summary**: A 3-sentence third-person summary:
   - *Sentence 1*: Title + YOE + positive adjective (*"Results-oriented Operations Manager with 12+ years experience..."*).
   - *Sentence 2*: Core competencies matching job posting keywords.
   - *Sentence 3*: Values and commitment (*"Committed to continuous improvement and team success."*).
3. **Career Highlights**: Top 2-4 quantified lifetime achievements or summary lines placed above work experience.
4. **Key Skills / Areas of Expertise**: 10-16 keywords (1-3 words each) lifted directly from job posting dissection.

### Page Count Guidelines
- **1 Page**: Students, recent graduates, or candidates with under 5 years of experience.
- **2 Pages**: Standard for **90%+** of mid-career professionals and managers.
- **3+ Pages**: C-Suite Executives or Academics/Researchers only.

### Section Ordering
- **Entry Level / Career Changers**: Contact → Headline → Summary → Key Skills → Education → Relevant Experience / Projects → Technical Proficiencies.
- **Experienced Professionals**: Contact → Headline → Summary → Key Skills → Career Highlights → Professional Experience → Education → Certifications / Additional.

### ATS (Applicant Tracking System) Rules
1. Use standard clean layouts (avoid text boxes, tables for core text, graphics, or multi-column resume builders that break parsing).
2. Use standard section headers (*"Professional Experience"*, *"Education"*, *"Volunteer Experience"*).
3. Never use headers/footers for key contact info (some ATS scanners strip headers/footers).
4. Do not justify text if it creates huge character gaps.
5. Standard fonts only: Calibri, Arial, Times New Roman, Tahoma. Body font size: 10–11pt.

## 5. The Executive Hybrid Cover Letter Strategy

### Anatomy of a Hybrid Cover Letter (6 Parts)
1. **Branding Header**: Matches the visual design/header of the resume.
2. **Address & Salutation**: Date, company info, salutation (*"Dear [Hiring Manager Name]"*, *"Dear Hiring Manager"*, or *"Dear Selection Committee"*). **Never use "To whom it may concern"**.
3. **Introductory Paragraph (3–5 Sentences)**: State role & company applied for, why applying, brief value summary, and introduce the body bullet points.
4. **Body Bullet Points (The Hybrid Bullet Layout)**: 4–6 scannable bullet points targeting top required skills:
   - **Headline** (Skill Name in **Bold**) + 2–4 line story/proof using one of 3 approaches:
     - *Specific Example*: Single impressive accomplishment.
     - *Summary of Experience*: Highlighting years of proficiency.
     - *Values / Priority*: Explaining commitment to that skill domain.
5. **Closing Paragraph**: Reiterate interest in company values/reputation, express gratitude, call to action.
6. **Sign Off**: *"Sincerely,"* + Printed Name.

### Why Bullet Points in Cover Letters?
- **Scannability**: Busy hiring managers scan cover letters in 5–10 seconds.
- **Undeniable Proof**: Directly maps top employer requirements to concrete proof.
- **Time Saving**: Easy to customize per application using a reusable "Bullet Point Library".

## 6. The 3TC Networking Strategy (Earning Decision Maker Trust)

Hiring is risky for employers. To mitigate risk, hiring managers prioritize candidates from **The 3 Trustworthy Categories (3TC)**:
1. **Internal Candidates**
2. **People They Know From Elsewhere**
3. **Candidates Recommended by Someone They Trust**

### Networking & Outreach Workflows

#### Scenario A: You Know the Hiring Manager
- Reach out **before applying** for a 10-15 minute informational chat to ask about role scope and culture (listening/learning mode, not pitching).
- Send a thank-you email, then submit application.

#### Scenario B: You Have a Mutual Connection
- Ask mutual contact for a warm email introduction to the hiring manager.
- **Reply immediately (within minutes)** using "Reply All" to thank the contact and request a brief chat with the hiring manager.

#### Scenario C: Cold Outreach (No Prior Contact)
1. Build a target list of 3-5 professionals/hiring managers in your field via LinkedIn (use "Posts" search filter to find hiring managers), company directories, or TheOrg.com.
2. Send a short connection ask (requesting a 10-15 min phone/video chat to learn about their career path).
3. **Informational Interview Framework**:
   - Prepare 10 questions, plan to ask 4-6.
   - *Goal 1*: Learn (career path, role duties, company culture).
   - *Goal 2*: Make a friend (be a good listener, show genuine interest).
   - *Goal 3*: Get help (ask for introductions to others, recommendations, or job leads).
   - *Goal 4*: Follow up with a personalized thank-you note within hours.

## 7. Collaborative AI Prompting Framework (ChatGPT / AI Strategy)

When using AI to draft or polish resumes and cover letters, follow the **"You + Human Coach + AI"** collaborative workflow:

### Key AI Rules & Forbidden Words
- **Do not ask AI to write an entire resume at once**. Build it block by block.
- **Eliminate Robot Buzzwords**: Watch out for and replace telltale AI words like *"Meticulously"*, *"Pioneered"*, *"Prowess"*, *"Realm"*, *"Helm"*, *"Spearheaded"* (when overused), and *"Testament"*.

### 7-Step Prompt Sequence
1. **Prompt 1 (Persona & JDs)**: Feed 2-3 target job descriptions and instruct AI to adopt the hiring manager persona.
2. **Prompt 2 (Key Skills)**: Request top 25 key hard skills (1-3 words each).
3. **Prompt 3 (Professional Summary)**: 3-sentence summary in 3rd person based on experience, values, and top achievement.
4. **Prompt 4 (Job Overviews)**: 1-sentence context line per role.
5. **Prompt 5 (Bullet Points)**: Generate bullets one at a time using raw user notes + quantified results.
6. **Prompt 6 (Gap Analysis)**: Ask AI to identify missing skills from current draft.
7. **Prompt 7 (Refining & Rewrites)**: Polish bullets for tone, conciseness, and action-verb leading statements.
