# Resume & Cover Letter Customization Playbook

An executive playbook for structuring ATS-optimized single-column resumes and scannable hybrid cover letters using quantified achievement formulas.

---

## 1. High-Impact Resume Bullet Point Formula

Every achievement bullet point follows a **3-part quantifiable formula**:

$$\text{High-Impact Bullet} = \text{Action Verb} + \text{Specific / Quantifiable Scope} + \text{Measurable Result}$$

### The 3 Formula Components
1. **Strong Action Verb**: Begin with a decisive, high-leverage verb (*Directed, Spearheaded, Architected, Engineered, Optimized, Negotiated, Replaced, Scaled*).
2. **Quantifiable Scope**: Define scale, team size, budget responsibility, platform volume, or geographic reach (*"managed a 12-person cross-functional team across 14 markets"*).
3. **Measurable Result**: State the tangible business outcome (*"resulting in +28% demo conversion uplift and $450K annual infrastructure cost savings"*).

### Executive Bullet Guidelines
* **No Speculative or Fabricated Metrics**: Use exact historical metrics when available. If exact numbers are confidential, use relative percentages or qualitative ranges (*"boosted self-serve conversion rate by +35%"*).
* **Zero Em Dash Policy (`—`, `&mdash;`)**: Em dashes are strictly prohibited. Use standard hyphens (` - `) or vertical pipes (` | `) as separators.
* **Tense Uniformity**: Use past-tense verbs for prior roles and completed milestones; use present-tense verbs for active ongoing responsibilities.

---

## 2. ATS (Applicant Tracking System) Formatting Rules

1. **Single-Column Standard Layout**: Avoid multi-column sidebars, text boxes, tables for core text, or embedded graphics that cause ATS parsing errors.
2. **Standard Section Headers**: Use clean, recognizable headings (*"Professional Experience"*, *"Areas of Expertise"*, *"Education"*, *"Certifications"*).
3. **Standard Typography**: Body font size 10–11pt using universal fonts (Calibri, Arial, Georgia, Times New Roman).
4. **Header Safety**: Keep primary contact details (Name, City/State, Phone, Email, LinkedIn) in the main document body rather than header/footer fields to prevent scanner stripping.

---

## 3. The 6-Part Hybrid Cover Letter Framework

### Structure & Layout
1. **Branding Header**: Mirrors the styling and contact info of the primary resume.
2. **Formal Recipient & Salutation**: Include date, target company name, and a targeted salutation (*"Dear [Company Name] Selection Committee"* or *"Dear [Hiring Manager Name]"*).
3. **Opening Positioning Hook (3–4 Sentences)**: State the specific role targeted, express genuine alignment with the company's technical trajectory, and summarize core value.
4. **Scannable Proof Bullets (Hybrid Format)**: 3–5 bolded headline bullet points mapping your specific background directly to key job requirements:
   * **[Core Competency / Skill Name]**: Brief 2–3 line narrative detailing a concrete achievement, team size, or platform outcome.
5. **Closing Executive Commitment**: Reiterate enthusiasm for organizational goals, express readiness for technical or strategic discussions, and thank the reader.
6. **Professional Sign-Off**: *"Sincerely,"* followed by printed name.
