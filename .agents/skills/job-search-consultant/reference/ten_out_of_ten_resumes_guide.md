# The Big Book of 10/10 Resumes Guide (By Executive Resume Strategists)

## The 10-Second Scan Reality Check
Hiring managers spend **5 to 10 seconds** skimming a resume before deciding to review it closely or reject it. A 10/10 resume passes this initial visual and keyword scan effortlessly by presenting clear professional branding up top.

---

## 1. The Resume Writing Mindset

1. **You Are the Product**: Think of yourself as a high-value service, and your resume as your main brochure.
2. **Filter Advice**: There is no single "perfect" font or format. Find an ATS-compliant, highly readable layout and stick to it.
3. **Build a Highlight Reel**: Focus on achievements and impact rather than exhausting, passive duty lists.

---

## 2. Market Research & Keyword Optimization

### Extracting Keywords from Job Postings
Before drafting, map out the job posting's:
- **Job Duties & Hard Skills**: Specific software, technical processes, certifications, operational duties.
- **Work Behaviors**: Intangibles (*fast-paced, high-stakes, collaborative, empathetic*).

Use these extracted terms across your summary, key skills section, and bullet points.

---

## 3. Resume Branding Elements (Top of Page 1)

### A. Professional Title / Headline
- Clearly label yourself using one of 4 approaches:
  1. *Exact Role Title*: `"Forensic Accountant"`
  2. *Summary of Expertise*: `"Accounting Expert"` or `"Seasoned Financial Strategist"`
  3. *Recent Grad / Career Changer*: `"Digital Marketing Enthusiast"` or `"HR Specialist"`
  4. *Strong Trait Title*: `"Relationship-Oriented Leader"`

### B. Professional Summary (3-Sentence Formula)
- Written in third person (no "I" or "my"):
  - **Sentence 1**: Title + YOE + core adjective (*"Accomplished Director with 10+ years experience leading HR operations..."*).
  - **Sentence 2**: Core functional capabilities matched to job posting.
  - **Sentence 3**: Professional values/commitment (*"Committed to continuous improvement and organizational growth."*).

### C. Career Highlights
- 2-4 headline style summary bullets placed above work experience to showcase your most impressive lifetime metrics (*"Efficiency Increase: Reduced warehouse processing time by 10%"*).

### D. Areas of Expertise / Key Skills
- 10-16 keywords (1-3 words each) organized in a clean 3-4 column grid (*"Budget Management", "Vendor Negotiations", "Lean Operations"*).

---

## 4. Structuring Education & Experience

### Education Section Rules
- **Degree Title**: Full official title (*Master of Science in College Student Affairs*).
- **Institution & Location**: School name, city, state/province.
- **Dates**: Graduation year (omit dates for degrees earned >15 years ago; list expected completion year for in-progress degrees).
- **Recent Grads (<2 YOE)**: May include GPA (if impressive), relevant coursework, capstone projects, or honors.

### Volunteer Experience
- **Type 1 ("Working without pay")**: Complex roles (e.g., Student Council President, Club VP). Format like a job with bullet points.
- **Type 2 ("Good person volunteering")**: Simple 1-line bullet entries.

### Additional Content Sections
- **Technical Proficiencies**: Software, languages, platforms, specialized equipment.
- **Certifications & Training**: PMP, CPR, Lean Six Sigma, AWS, etc.
- **Languages / Publications / Presentations**.

---

## 5. Top 9 Resume Formatting & ATS Rules

1. **Page Length**: 1 page for students/grads (<5 YOE); **2 pages for 90%+ of mid-career candidates**; 3+ pages for C-suite/academics.
2. **Page-to-Page Continuity**: If a job role spans across pages, append `(Continued on page 2)` at bottom of page 1 and `[Job Title] at [Company] Continued` at top of page 2.
3. **Simple Fonts**: Calibri, Times New Roman, Arial, or Tahoma (10-11pt body text). Max 2 fonts.
4. **Clean Margins & Spacing**: Use blank line spacing between sections.
5. **ATS Scanner Rules**:
   - Avoid graphics, text boxes, and complex multi-layer columns.
   - Use standard headers (*"Professional Experience"*, *"Education"*, *"Volunteer Experience"*).
   - Keep contact info in the document body (not in Word document headers/footers).
   - Do not justify text alignment if it causes wide word gaps.
   - List experience in reverse chronological order.
6. **No-No's**:
   - ❌ No headshot photos (unless applying outside North America or acting/modeling).
   - ❌ No first-person pronouns ("I", "me", "my").
   - ❌ Omit "References Available Upon Request" (it is implied).
   - ❌ Never lie or exaggerate.
