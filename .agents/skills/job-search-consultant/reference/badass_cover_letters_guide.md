# Big Book of Badass Cover Letters Guide (By Executive Resume Strategists)

## Why Cover Letters Still Matter
Even though many hiring managers do not read every cover letter initially, cover letters serve 5 vital purposes:
1. **Prevents "Easy Elimination"**: Avoids being discarded in round 1 for failing to follow application instructions.
2. **Read by Decision Makers for Top Candidates**: When narrowing down top 5-10 applicants, managers read cover letters to differentiate finalists.
3. **Demonstrates Written Communication Skills**: Proves clear, structured writing ability.
4. **Satisfies Traditional Hiring Managers**: Protects against penalties from conservative reviewers.
5. **Zero Risk**: Takes only 5 minutes when using the Hybrid Bullet Point method.

---

## Cover Letter Golden Rules
1. **Strict 1-Page Limit**: Anything longer is a letter, not a cover letter.
2. **Role & Company Specific**: Mention the exact job title and company name at least twice.
3. **High Scannability**: Use bullet points instead of 4-5 dense paragraphs.

---

## The Anatomy of a Badass Cover Letter (6 Key Elements)

```
[ 1. Branding Header (Matches Resume Design) ]
[ 2. Address & Date & Salutation ]
[ 3. Introductory Paragraph (3-5 sentences) ]
[ 4. Body Bullet Points (4-6 Hybrid Bullets with Headlines) ]
[ 5. Closing Paragraph (3-4 sentences + Call to Action) ]
[ 6. Sign Off & Name ]
```

### 1. Branding Header
- Ensure the header styling (Name, Title, Contact Info, Font, Line dividers) matches your resume header **exactly**.

### 2. Addressing the Cover Letter
- **Salutation Options**:
  - `Dear [Hiring Manager First & Last Name],` (Preferred if known)
  - `Dear Hiring Manager,` (Standard & safe for most roles)
  - `Dear Selection Committee,` (Best for senior/executive/academic roles)
- **NEVER USE**: *"To whom it may concern"* (dated and impersonal).

### 3. Introductory Paragraph Templates

#### Option A: Greg's Go-To Intro
> *"I am excited to submit my application for this [JOB TITLE] position at [COMPANY NAME]. I found this job posting online and after comparing the responsibilities to my own skills and interests, I jumped at the chance to apply. I believe my [#] years’ experience in [JOB TITLE/FIELD] have allowed me to develop the critical skills needed to excel in this position. In my resume, as well as the page below, I have taken the time to highlight some of my specific experiences that have prepared me to be a strong contributor to your team in this [JOB TITLE] position."*

#### Option B: The Enthusiastic Intro
> *"I am beyond thrilled to submit my application for this incredible [JOB TITLE] opportunity at [COMPANY NAME]. I have been searching for an opportunity like this for months and the moment I saw this, I knew I had to apply. I wholeheartedly believe that my professional background and education in [INDUSTRY] have prepared me to be an exceptional contributor to your team and I have taken the time to elaborate on that below."*

#### Option C: The Highly Professional Intro
> *"I am writing to formally submit my application for the [JOB TITLE] position at [COMPANY NAME]. Enclosed you will find my resume which details my [#] years of progressive experience in [INDUSTRY] where I had the opportunity to develop the essential skills required to thrive in this role. Please see the bullet points below as illustrations of my relevant capabilities which have positioned me to add substantial value to your organization in this [JOB TITLE] position."*

#### Option D: Referencing a Referral Intro
> *"I am excited to submit my application for this [JOB TITLE] position at [COMPANY NAME]. A former colleague and friend of mine, [REFEREE NAME], who works with your organization as a [THEIR JOB TITLE], recommended that I apply to this position, and I am so pleased [HE/SHE/THEY] did because this role seems like an excellent fit. I believe my [#] years’ experience in [JOB TITLE] have allowed me to develop the critical skills needed to excel in this position and I have elaborated on my relevant skillsets below."*

#### Option E: Applying Internally Intro
> *"I am excited at the prospect of advancing my career here at [COMPANY NAME] in a new capacity as the [JOB TITLE]. As you are aware, I have spent the last [#] years developing an extensive knowledge of our processes and building exceptional relationships with our incredible teams. I believe that foundation, combined with my highly relevant professional skillset (detailed below) make me an outstanding choice for this role."*

## 4. Body Bullet Points (The Hybrid Bullet Method)

Select **4 to 6 core topics** based on the job posting dissection. Each bullet point consists of a **Bolded Headline** followed by a 2-4 line narrative using one of three writing styles:

### Style 1: Specific Example (Best)
- Focuses on a single impressive milestone or story.
- *Example*: **Cost Savings**  -  *In my Operations Manager role with ABC Company, I restructured employee workflow to reduce production bottlenecks, decreasing cost per unit by 14%.*

### Style 2: Summary of Experience (Strong)
- Highlights total scope and longevity of skill mastery.
- *Example*: **Staff Leadership**  -  *I have 14+ years of experience directing cross-functional teams of 10-25 staff to execute projects and day-to-day operations in high-stakes hospitality settings.*

### Style 3: Values / Priority (Fallback)
- Explains philosophy when specific metric or long story isn't available.
- *Example*: **Customer Service**  -  *I place an exceptionally high priority on customer service in my work. By building trust with customers and providing exceptional service, I have been able to consistently retain customers at a high rate.*

## 5. Closing Paragraph Templates

#### Option A: Greg's Go-To Closing
> *"Finally, I am thrilled at the prospect of joining such an impressive organization. [COMPANY NAME] has a great reputation and strong values that closely align with my own. I would be grateful for the chance to contribute to your future success. Thank you very much for reviewing my application and I look forward to further communication."*

#### Option B: The Enthusiastic Closing
> *"Before wrapping up this cover letter, I want to reiterate what an exciting opportunity this is. I have always dreamed of working for [COMPANY NAME] and I am so enthused by the prospect of realizing that dream. You do outstanding work, and I would be incredibly proud to contribute to your continued success. Thank you very much for reviewing my application and I look forward to connecting with you."*

#### Option C: Highly Professional Closing
> *"Finally, I wanted to share my immense respect for your organization. You do important work, and I would be honored to attach my reputation to the [COMPANY NAME] name. I appreciate your time in reviewing my application, and I hope to be hearing from you shortly regarding next steps in the process."*

---

## 6. Cover Letter Writing Checklist
- [ ] Select template matching resume header visually.
- [ ] Pick intro & closing paragraphs matching desired tone.
- [ ] Review job description to extract top 4-6 required skills.
- [ ] Draft hybrid bullet points with bold headlines + quantified impact.
- [ ] Save bullets to your personal "Bullet Point Library" for future applications.
- [ ] Verify salutation and sign off (*Sincerely, [Your Name]*).
- [ ] Proofread for zero typos and strictly enforce the 1-page boundary.
