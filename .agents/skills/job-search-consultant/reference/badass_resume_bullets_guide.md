# 540 Badass Bullet Points for Your Resume Guide (By Executive Resume Strategists)

## What Makes a Bullet Point "Badass"?

A standard bullet point describes duties passively:
❌ *Planned and executed events for corporate clients at the conference center.*

A **Badass Bullet Point** demonstrates scale, specific actions, context, tools, and impact:
✅ *Planned and executed 30+ events per year on budgets of up to $200K for corporate clients such as TeleCo and GTS Bank, overseeing logistics, event staff, registration, and catering for groups of 200-500 guests at the 18,000 sq ft conference center.*

---

## The 3-Part Bullet Point Formula

$$\text{Action Verb} + \text{Specific / Quantifiable Detail} + \text{Measurable Result}$$

1. **Action Verb**: Starts with a high-impact verb (e.g., *Spearheaded, Streamlined, Oversaw, Generated*).
2. **Specific / Quantifiable Detail**: Details scope, tools, numbers, team sizes, budgets, or frequency.
3. **Measurable Result**: Clear output or ROI (*"reducing processing time by 25%"*, *"generating $1.2M in annual revenue"*).

## Bullet Point Rules & No-No's

### 1. Never Lie or Fabricate Numbers
- If you don't know the exact percentage, **do not make up a metric**. Use a qualitative descriptor.
  - ❌ *Yielded a 98% account retention rate.* (If made up)
  - ✅ *Yielded above-expected retention rates across all regional markets.*

### 2. Protect Confidentiality
- If bound by NDA or internal sensitivity:
  - Substitute company names for industry terms (*"Supported a major client in automotive"*).
  - Substitute exact dollar amounts for percentage increases or ranges (*"Managed a 7-figure operating budget"*).

### 3. Verb Tense Rules
- **Past Roles / Completed Projects**: Always use **Past Tense** (*Managed, Created, Delivered*).
- **Current Roles (Recurring Tasks)**: Use **Present Tense** (*Manage, Lead, Direct*).

- **Customer Success**: *Decreased time-to-value from 4-8 months down to 30 days, facilitating a 3-tiered implementation process, providing high-touch onboarding for clients.*
- **Leadership Development**: *Coached 200+ executives on personal performance improvement, people-oriented leadership, positive culture building, and guiding growth among team members.*

- **Project Management**: *Project managed an 8-figure construction project to facilitate the launch of the first Arizona-based franchise, completing the build on-time and on budget.*
