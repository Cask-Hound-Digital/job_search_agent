# The ChatGPT Resume Guide & Prompts (By Executive Resume Strategists)

## Core Philosophy: Collaborative AI Resume Writing
AI (like ChatGPT or Gemini) is a powerful assistant, but **cannot write a great resume in isolation**. A great resume requires a 3-way partnership:

```
[ You (Career Context & Critical Thinking) ]
                       +
[ Human Strategy (Greg's Frameworks & Prompts) ]
                       +
[ AI Tool (Drafting, Speed, & Keyword Optimization) ]
```

---

## Major Mistakes to Avoid When Using AI

1. **Do Not Input Unverifiable Information**: AI may hallucinate accomplishments or numbers. Review every word carefully.
2. **Do Not Ask for the Whole Resume at Once**: Asking AI to write an entire resume in one prompt generates generic, low-quality fluff. Build piece by piece.
3. **Eliminate Robotic Buzzwords**: AI overuses telltale words that alienate hiring managers:
   - ❌ *Meticulously*
   - ❌ *Pioneered*
   - ❌ *Prowess*
   - ❌ *Realm*
   - ❌ *Helm*
   - ❌ *Spearheaded* (when repeated continuously)
   - ❌ *Testament*

## The 7-Step Step-by-Step Prompt Sequence

### Prompt 1: Training AI as a Hiring Manager
Copy/paste this first prompt along with 2-3 target job descriptions:
> *"I am writing a resume for a few jobs, and I need you to play the part of the hiring manager to help me make my resume perfect for this job. Below, I have attached a couple of job descriptions that represent the types of jobs I am applying to. After this prompt, I’ll start to ask you questions and provide you information to help me build my resume. Let me know if you understand and here are the job postings: [INSERT JOB DESCRIPTIONS]"*

### Prompt 2: Extracting Key Hard Skills
> *"Based on the jobs I provided you; can you please provide a list of the 25 most important skills required to do these jobs well. Please make each skill 1-3 words long and focus more on hard skills over soft skills."*

### Prompt 3: Drafting the Professional Summary
Fill in your details and send:
> *"Based on the requirements for the job and the information below regarding my values, skills, and career highlights, can you please write me a short concise professional summary that is 3 sentences long to add to my resume? Please make the tone direct, professional, and do not write in first person. Here is the additional information:
> - I have [#] years of experience in [Industry]
> - I value [1-2 core professional values]
> - My best skills are [2-3 skills from Prompt 2]
> - One of my best accomplishments was [brief description of a major achievement]"*

### Prompt 4: Writing Job Overviews (Context Lines)
> *"Can you please provide me with a one sentence overview for my resume that provides context about this job? This should be short, direct, and professional in tone. Here is an example of the structure I’m looking for: 'Recruited by the [type/size of company] to take on responsibilities including [key duties]'. Here is some information about the job: [INSERT COMPANY TYPE, SIZE, & DUTIES]"*

### Prompt 5: Generating Badass Bullet Points (One at a time)
> *"Can you please write me a resume bullet point based on the information below? I would like this to be direct and professional with a focus on accomplishments. Also please use the job descriptions I have provided to ensure that we’re aligning with the key skills the hiring manager is looking for. Here is an example of the type of bullet point I am looking for: 'Achieved a 20% cost savings on shipping by negotiating a new long-term deal with a key vendor.' And here is the information I would like you to create the bullet point based upon: [INSERT RAW ACCOMPLISHMENT / NOTES]"*

### Prompt 6: Gap Analysis & Extra Ideas
> *"Below are all the bullet points I have under this [Job Title] job so far. Based on my experience and also the key skills that you would be looking for as a hiring manager, are there any other skills or experiences that you think I could speak to with bullet points for this job? If so, please provide me with a list of 3-5 skills you think I could add bullet points about: [INSERT DRAFT JOB SECTION]"*

### Prompt 7: Refining Existing Bullets
> *"Can you please rewrite these resume bullet points to be more exciting and professional with a focus on accomplishments? [INSERT EXISTING BULLETS]"*

## Prompts 8–19: The Rewrite Toolkit

Use these targeted feedback prompts to fine-tune any draft output:

8. *Can you please rewrite that to make it shorter?*
9. *Can you please rewrite that to sound more direct and professional?*
10. *Can you please rewrite that and remove unnecessary adjectives like "Remarkable" and "Meticulous"?*
11. *Can you please rewrite that but put the accomplishment at the beginning of the sentence?*
12. *Can you please rewrite that and remove first-person / third-person language from the content?*
13. *Can you please rewrite that to sound more exciting and impressive?*
14. *Can you please rewrite that and replace uncommon language like "pioneered" and "helmed"?*
15. *Can you please rewrite that with a different approach so I can see another option?*
16. *Can you please rewrite that and integrate a couple of the key skills you provided me earlier?*
17. *Can you please rewrite that to sound more like the examples I provided?*
18. *Can you please rewrite that to make me sound more like an executive?*
19. *I didn’t do [X], can you please rewrite the last line to exclude any mention of that?*

---

## Prompts 20–24: Expanding to Full Job Search

20. **Cover Letter Generation**: *Can you please use the job descriptions I have provided and all the resume information I gave you to write a professional and direct cover letter I can use to go with the resume?*
21. **LinkedIn / DM Intro**: *Can you please draft a short introductory message for me that I can send to the hiring manager to tell them that I’m interested in this role and that I think I would be a great fit?*
22. **Interview Question Prediction**: *Can you please provide me with a list of interview questions you would ask if you were hiring for the jobs I shared earlier?*
23. **Informational Interview Request**: *Can you please write a short blurb I can use to ask someone at the company I’m applying to if they would be willing to talk to me for a few minutes over the phone so I can learn more about what it’s like to work there?*
24. **Post-Interview Thank You Email**: *Can you please write me a template for a "Thank You" email that I can send to the hiring manager after the interview that recaps the value I can bring to their company?*
